WebCompass: Towards Multimodal Web Coding Evaluation for Code Language Models

Xinping Lei, Xinyu Che, Junqi Xiong, Chenchen Zhang, Yukai Huang, et al.

Evaluating multimodal web development across generation, editing, and repair, with an Agent-as-a-Judge that tests websites in a real browser.面向网页生成、编辑与修复的多模态评测，结合真实浏览器交互与 Agent-as-a-Judge 自动化验收。

1,526 tasks · 7 task categories · Human agreement r = 0.93–0.961,526 个任务 · 7 类任务 · 与人工评分相关系数 r = 0.93–0.96

AVE-Compass: Towards Holistic Evaluation for Audio-Video Editing Abilities

Yuqing Wen, Yukai Huang, Qianqian Xie, Jiangtao Wu, Yibin Lin, et al.

A benchmark and modular agent for audio-video editing, connecting dependency-aware planning, tool execution, and evaluator feedback.音视频协同编辑基准与模块化智能体，连接依赖感知规划、工具执行与评估反馈。

AVE-Agent vs. Wan2.7: Editing Intent 42.4 → 59.8 (+17.4 points)AVE-Agent 相较 Wan2.7：编辑意图得分 42.4 → 59.8（+17.4 分）

Paper ↗Project ↗
My contribution我的贡献 +

I built the evaluation dataset and contributed to the benchmark and AVE-Agent architecture. The system uses Gemini 2.5 Flash for planning and tools including Wan2.7, SAM Audio, and MMAudio V2. I designed and ran major experiments, analyzed results, and contributed to the methods and experiments sections.负责评测数据集构建，参与 Benchmark 与 AVE-Agent 架构设计。系统采用 Gemini 2.5 Flash 进行规划，组合调用 Wan2.7、SAM Audio 和 MMAudio V2 等工具；负责主要实验设计、执行与结果分析，参与方法和实验部分撰写。
